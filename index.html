One loop, six steps, no guessing

The same method runs on every account, which is why it transfers to an in-house team.

  1. Market & competitor intel

    Every competitor's ads on every platform, search demand and real customer pain points.

  2. Audience & offer

    Micro-segments, each with one sharp offer and one message it can believe.

  3. Funnel & media plan

    Channels matched to intent, and a budget split that moves from test to scale.

  4. Tracking before spend

    GTM, GA4, Pixel and CAPI, plus call and WhatsApp events, live before launch.

  5. Creative testing

    ABO to find winning hooks and audiences, CBO to scale what the data proves.

  6. KPI loop

    Weekly CPL and CPA reviews; budget follows whatever converts.
